Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using the HARA recruitment portal.

How do I create a professional profile?
Simply click on the "Register" button in the navigation bar, fill in your details, and upload your resume. A complete profile increases your chances of being noticed by recruiters.
Is HARA free for students?
Yes! HARA is completely free for students and job seekers. You can browse, apply, and connect with companies at no cost.
How can I track my applications?
Once you apply for a job, you will receive a confirmation message. Our team or the hiring company will contact you directly via email for further steps.
Can I apply for multiple roles?
Absolutely. You can apply for as many roles as you feel qualified for. Each application is treated independently by the hiring teams.
